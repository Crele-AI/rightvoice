Scoring uses Whisper (MIT) for recognition, Silero VAD (MIT) for activity, a SpeechBrain ECAPA
speaker encoder (Apache-2.0) for voice match, and DNSMOS P.808 from Microsoft's DNS Challenge
repository, whose content is licensed CC BY 4.0, for quality. The scorer reads the DNSMOS model
from that repository at run time and does not redistribute it.

Device and codec stages use ffmpeg (libopus, opencore-amr, G.711 mu-law). The ffmpeg build and the
library versions are recorded in every build's `build_info.json`.

## What is not in RightVoice

Sources were kept out when their licence does not allow commercial use or redistribution, or when
they are widely used to train speech enhancement and separation models, so that a scored system is
less likely to have heard them. The manifest names every source used
and nothing else.
